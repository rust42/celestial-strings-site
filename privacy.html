Privacy Policy

Last updated August 27, 2026

Celestial Strings does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every feature runs entirely on your device.

Microphone access

Oracle, the tuner, uses your device's microphone to detect the pitch of your instrument in real time. That audio is processed on-device only — it is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and is discarded immediately after each analysis.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads

Celestial Strings has no user accounts, no sign-in, and does not track you or your usage in any way. It shows no advertising.

No network access

Celestial Strings makes no network requests of any kind. It works fully offline.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this repository.
